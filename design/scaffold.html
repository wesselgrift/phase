Design scaffold

phase.

A calm period tracker with a daily check-in and hormone-based insights. The style is inspired by the Stoic journaling app. Everything below uses the app's real CSS and markup, rendered with sample data.

Brief for restyling
  • Mobile first: screens are 390px wide. Keep touch targets at 44px or more.
  • Keep the class names, so the new styles can be copied back into index.html.
  • Every color is a token on :root, with dark values under [data-theme=dark]. Light theme is used in the day and dark in the evening.
  • The five phase colors carry meaning (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal, late luteal). They passed colorblind and contrast checks on both themes. If you change them, keep neighbours easy to tell apart.
  • Text always uses the ink tokens. Phase colors appear only on marks: dots, ring, bars, calendar fills.
  • Tone: calm and warm. The app leads with what is on her side in each phase, not with symptoms.
